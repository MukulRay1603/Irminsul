# RAYY team notes: public facts (checked 2026-10-26)

## 1. Wishes

- On the Character Event Wish and the Standard Wish, the 5-star base rate is 0.6% and the official consolidated rate (pity included) is 1.6%; a 25-million-pull community dataset measured 1.605%, which agrees with it. | https://www.hoyolab.com/article/497840 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- On the Weapon Event Wish (Epitome Invocation), the 5-star base rate is 0.7% and the official consolidated rate is 1.85%. | https://www.hoyolab.com/article/497840 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- The 4-star base rate is 5.1% (13% consolidated) on character and standard wishes, and 6% (14.5% consolidated) on the weapon wish. | https://www.hoyolab.com/article/497840 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- One wish costs one Intertwined Fate (event wishes) or one Acquaint Fate (Standard Wish), and either Fate can be bought for 160 Primogems. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Character Event Wishes guarantee a 5-star within 90 wishes, and the Weapon Event Wish guarantees one within 80. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- HoYoverse has never published "soft pity"; the term and its starting points come from community analysis of large pull datasets, not from official text. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Community data (estimate, not official) puts 5-star soft pity at about pull 74 on character and standard wishes, with the rate rising by about 6 percentage points per pull from there until pull 90. | https://www.hoyolab.com/article/497840 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Community data (estimate, not official) puts weapon-wish soft pity at about pull 63, with the rate rising by about 7 percentage points per pull and reaching 100% by pull 77. | https://library.keqingmains.com/evidence/general-mechanics/gacha | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Community data (estimate, not official) puts 4-star soft pity at about the 9th wish on character and standard wishes and the 8th wish on the weapon wish. | https://www.hoyolab.com/article/497840 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- On a Character Event Wish, each 5-star has a 50% chance of being the promotional character; if it is not, the next 5-star is guaranteed to be the promotional character, so the worst case is 180 wishes. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Winning the 50/50 does not make the next 5-star guaranteed: the next one is a 50/50 again. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Pity count and the guarantee carry over to the next banner of the same wish type, but never between the character, weapon and standard wishes. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Official notices state that "Character Event Wish" and "Character Event Wish-2" share one guarantee count, which is separate from every other wish type. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/166634 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Capturing Radiance is a Character Event Wish mechanic that arrived with Version 5.0. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/125553 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1 (since 5.0)
- HoYoverse's official Q&A (clarified on 10 February 2025) gives a base chance of 0.018% for Capturing Radiance to trigger. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/125274 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Counting the chance of Capturing Radiance, the official consolidated chance that a 5-star won on the event wish is the promotional character is 55%. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/125274 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Officially, if the promotional character was your second 5-star on three occasions in a row, Capturing Radiance is guaranteed to trigger on your next 5-star character. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/125274 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Capturing Radiance can only trigger when the next 5-star is not already guaranteed (that is, on a 50/50); it does not change pity or the guarantee. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Version 5.0 cut the number of Fate Points needed for the weapon wish's Epitomized Path from 2 to 1. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/125553 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1 (since 5.0)
- With Epitomized Path, any 5-star weapon other than the one you chose gives 1 Fate Point, and with that point your next 5-star is the chosen weapon, so the worst case is 160 wishes. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Fate Points reset when you get the chosen weapon, when you change or cancel your choice, or when the current Epitome Invocation ends; they never carry over to the next weapon banner. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- On the weapon wish, each 5-star has a 75% chance of being one of the two promotional weapons, and after an off-banner 5-star the next one is guaranteed to be promotional. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Character, weapon, Chronicled and Lightrace wishes all guarantee a 4-star or better item at least once in every 10 wishes. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Community data shows that a 5-star landing on the 10th wish does not reset the 4-star counter; the 4-star guarantee moves to the next wish. | https://www.hoyolab.com/article/497840 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- On a character banner, each 4-star has a 50% chance of being one of the featured 4-star characters; if it is not, the next 4-star is guaranteed to be featured. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Wish | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- In a Chronicled Wish you first pick one 5-star character or weapon as your Designated Item, and every 5-star from that wish will then be of the same kind (all characters or all weapons). | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/122695 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Officially, Chronicled Wish guarantees a 5-star within 90 wishes, each 5-star has a 50% chance of being the Designated Item, and a 4-star or better is guaranteed at least once in every 10 wishes. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/122695 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- In Chronicled Wish, a 5-star that is not your Designated Item gives 1 Fate Point (the maximum), and with it the next 5-star is guaranteed to be the Designated Item. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/122695 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- The Chronicled Wish guarantee count carries over to the next Chronicled Wish, but Fate Points reset to 0 when the event ends. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/122695 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Official notices state that Chronicled Wish and Lightrace Wish share one guarantee count, which is separate from every other wish type. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/165007 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Wishes give Masterless Starglitter back: 2 for a duplicate 4-star character or any 4-star weapon (5 once the character is fully constellated), and 10 for a duplicate 5-star character or any 5-star weapon (25 once the character is fully constellated). | https://game8.co/games/Genshin-Impact/archives/301796 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Every 3-star item from a wish gives 15 Masterless Stardust. | https://game8.co/games/Genshin-Impact/archives/301795 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- In Paimon's Bargains, an Intertwined or Acquaint Fate costs 5 Masterless Starglitter, with no monthly limit. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Paimon%27s_Bargains | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- In Paimon's Bargains, an Intertwined or Acquaint Fate costs 75 Masterless Stardust, with a limit of 5 of each per month (the shop resets on the 1st). | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Paimon%27s_Bargains | 2026-10-26 | 7.1

## 2. Adventure Rank and World Level

- The maximum Adventure Rank is 60 and the maximum World Level is 9. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Rank | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- World Level goes up at Adventure Rank 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45, 50, 55 and 58 (World Levels 1 to 9). | https://game8.co/games/Genshin-Impact/archives/297426 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- You need to complete an Ascension Quest to reach World Level 2 (AR 25), 4 (AR 35), 6 (AR 45), 7 (AR 50) and 9 (AR 58); the other World Levels come automatically. | https://game8.co/games/Genshin-Impact/archives/297426 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Adventure Rank cannot go past 25, 35, 45 or 50 until you finish that rank's Ascension Quest, but Adventure EXP keeps building up and applies once the quest is done. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Rank | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- The World Level 9 Ascension quest at AR 58 does not stop Adventure Rank from going up. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Rank | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Once you reach AR 60, any further Adventure EXP turns into Mora. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Rank | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Daily Commissions unlock at Adventure Rank 12, and Expeditions (dispatch) unlock at AR 14. | https://game8.co/games/Genshin-Impact/archives/304605 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Co-Op Mode unlocks at Adventure Rank 16. | https://game8.co/games/Genshin-Impact/archives/297418 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- To join someone's world in co-op, your World Level must be equal to or higher than the host's, except that a World Level 8 player can join a World Level 9 host. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Rank | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Spiral Abyss unlocks at Adventure Rank 20, and its entrance is the domain at Musk Reef. | https://www.icy-veins.com/genshin-impact/spiral-abyss | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Domains unlock by Adventure Rank: weapon materials (Forgery) at AR 16, artifacts (Blessing) at AR 22, and talent materials (Mastery) at AR 27. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Rank | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- The Serenitea Pot unlocks at Adventure Rank 28. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Rank | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Imaginarium Theater officially requires Adventure Rank 35 or above and completion of the World Quest "Unbegun, Unending Story". | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/123975 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1 (rule from 4.7)
- Character and weapon ascension phases 1 to 6 need Adventure Rank 15, 25, 30, 35, 40 and 50, which raise the level cap to 40, 50, 60, 70, 80 and 90. | https://genshin-impact.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Rank | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Since Version 4.7, the Abyssal Moon Spire (Spiral Abyss Floors 9 to 12) resets on the 16th of every month, and earning 9 stars on a floor gives 200 Primogems. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/123975 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1 (since 4.7)

## 3. Primogems

- In the official 7.1 update notice, maintenance compensation is 300 Primogems, described as 60 Primogems per hour the servers are down. The maintenance was estimated at 5 hours. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/166383 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- The official 7.0 update notice used the same wording (300 Primogems, 60 per hour of downtime), so 300 per version update is the current norm. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/165655 | 2026-10-26 | 7.0-7.1
- Version update notices also list a separate "Issue Fix Compensation" of 300 Primogems, which in 7.1 had to be claimed within about 3 days of the update (by 2026/09/26 06:00 UTC+8). | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/166383 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Update compensation goes to Travelers at Adventure Rank 5 or above. It arrives by in-game mail within 5 hours after maintenance ends, and the mail expires after 30 days. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/166383 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- The official 7.1 Benefits Overview lists 10 Intertwined Fates and 1,600 Primogems among the anniversary rewards. | https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/news/detail/166086 | 2026-10-26 | 7.1
- Estimate, not official: GachaHaven's planner (reviewed September 2026) puts an active free-to-play player at about 9,000 to 13,000 Primogems for a typical 6-week version, or about 13,000 to 17,000 with the Welkin Moon. | https://www.gachahaven.com/genshin/guides/primogems-per-patch | 2026-10-26 | 7.x (general estimate)
