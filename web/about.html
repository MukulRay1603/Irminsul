About

A Genshin assistant that shows its work

Irminsul is a free, unofficial Genshin Impact assistant. It answers from official game data, shows the source behind every answer, never discusses leaks, and has a story-safe mode so lore answers don't spoil quests you haven't played yet.

How it works

  1. Game data first. Stats, passives, constellations, set bonuses and other facts are answered straight from official game data, instantly, with no AI involved.
  2. AI only when needed. For questions that need explaining, an AI model writes an answer using only the official pages it was given, and has to cite them.
  3. Every sentence checked. Each sentence of an AI answer is compared with the source it cites. Anything that can't be matched is marked, so you can see what to double-check.
  4. A leak filter. Unreleased characters and content stay out, both from the questions it will take and from the data it answers with.
  5. Guardrails. Personal data, unsafe topics, cheats and attempts to trick the assistant are politely refused.

Curious how accurate it is? The public test results come from automated tests anyone can rerun.

Principles

  • Official data onlyAnswers come from the game's own data and official sources.
  • No leaksNothing unreleased, ever, however the question is asked.
  • Sources on every answerEach answer names where it came from, so you can check it.
  • Family-friendlyKind, safe and suitable for every Traveler.
  • Genshin onlyIt stays on topic and politely declines everything else.

Privacy

  • No accounts and no tracking. No sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies. (The page's font is loaded from Google Fonts.)
  • Your planner and story point stay on your device. They're saved in your browser, not on our server. The story point goes along with a question only to filter spoilers, and isn't kept.
  • The AI-answer counter is in memory only. It remembers your connection for a while so answers are shared fairly, and is forgotten after that or whenever the server restarts.
  • Feedback is anonymous. No name, email, IP address or ID is stored, times are rounded to the hour, and messages with personal details are refused rather than saved.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. Not now, not ever.

Fair use

Irminsul is free and runs on free tiers with no budget behind it, so AI-written answers are shared fairly:

  • Your AI answers refill like stamina, one at a time, so there's always more later.
  • A shared pool refills steadily all day instead of at midnight, so players in every time zone get a turn.
  • Answers from game data are always free: stats, passives, constellations, set bonuses and repeat questions never count.
  • A gentle speed limit stops automated spam. Normal play never comes close.
