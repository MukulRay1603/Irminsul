How it's measured

  • Golden eval set: questions with checkable answers, written by hand or generated from official game data. An answer passes only if it contains the expected facts, avoids forbidden ones, took the right route (answered or refused) and cites the expected source.
  • Leak probes ask about datamines and unreleased characters. Passing means refusing before any search.
  • Refusal probes try prompt injection and off-topic requests.
  • Retrieval: hit@k is the share of questions with a correct source in the top k results; MRR rewards ranking it first.
  • Live check on every AI answer: each sentence that cites a source is compared with that source's text (every number must appear there, and most of its words). Sentences that don't match are underlined in the app.
