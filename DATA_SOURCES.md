# Data sources and rights

What Irminsul reads, what each owner's terms say, and what is still unresolved. Checked on
6 November 2026. This is a plain record kept by the project, not legal advice.

Irminsul is free and non-commercial: no ads, payments or accounts. It is an unofficial fan
project, not affiliated with or endorsed by HoYoverse.

| Source | What we use | What its terms say | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| HoYoverse news and HoYoWiki | Notices, wish dates, character, weapon and quest pages | The [Terms of Service](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/company/terms) (updated 20 May 2026) say the services may not be copied, downloaded or scraped without COGNOSPHERE's express prior written permission | **No permission yet.** Read about twice a day, with a client that names this project |
| Yatta (Project Amber) | Game text: characters, weapons, artifacts, stories, books, quests | No terms or licence found on the site. The text itself belongs to HoYoverse, so the row above applies | **No permission yet** |
| Official art | Character and item images | Belongs to HoYoverse, as above | **No permission yet** |
| KQM Theorycrafting Library | Mechanics and theorycrafting pages | The [repository](https://github.com/KQM-git/TCL) states no licence. An earlier version of this project said CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; we could not confirm that, so the claim was removed | **Permission to be requested.** Credited and linked; can be turned off with one setting |
| KQM quick guides | Item names and stat priorities, for characters without a team build | The guides say "Please link this guide if you use it as a reference". robots.txt allows crawling | Linked on every use. No guide prose is stored |
| Game8, Icy Veins, Prydwen | Short build facts written by the team | Each line links its page. Game8's terms forbid copying its content | Facts in our own words, never copied. Not read by machine |
| Enka.Network | A player's public showcase, only when they enter their UID | Respects its `ttl`, sends a named client, never lists UIDs | Nothing is stored |
| YouTube Data API | Community video suggestions | Standard API terms | Links and embeds only |

## How the data is handled

- The full corpus stays in a private dataset. It is not published for download.
- Answers summarise and link. They are checked sentence by sentence against their sources.
- Only released content is served. Leaks are refused, and nothing comes from leak sources.
- Dialogue from quests is not stored.
- Wish history stays in the player's browser. Irminsul never asks for login details or authkey links.

## If you own something here

Use the contact form on the [About page](https://mukulray-irminsul.hf.space/about). Anything
you want changed or removed will be, and a source can be switched off in the next data run.
